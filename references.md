# REFERENCES & ACADEMIC SOURCES

### 1. Primary Textbooks & Theoretical Foundations
1. **Strang, Gilbert.** *Introduction to Linear Algebra.* 5th ed., Wellesley-Cambridge Press, 2016.
   - Chapter 2: "Solving Linear Equations" – Detailed analysis of Gaussian Elimination, Augmented Matrices, Pivots, and Row Echelon Form.
   - Section 2.2: The Idea of Elimination; Section 2.3: Elimination Using Matrices.
2. **Lay, David C., Lay, Steven R., and McDonald, Judi J.** *Linear Algebra and Its Applications.* 5th ed., Pearson, 2015.
   - Chapter 1: "Linear Equations in Linear Algebra" – Row reduction, echelon forms, geometric interpretation of 3D intersection points.
3. **Anton, Howard, and Rorres, Chris.** *Elementary Linear Algebra: Applications Version.* 11th ed., Wiley, 2013.
   - Section 1.1 & 1.2: Systems of Linear Equations and Gaussian Elimination algorithm, Back-Substitution proofs.
4. **Golub, Gene H., and Van Loan, Charles F.** *Matrix Computations.* 4th ed., Johns Hopkins University Press, 2013.
   - Section 3.1 & 3.2: Gaussian Elimination, roundoff error, and row swapping (partial pivoting) for numerical stability.

### 2. Geometric & Visual Pedagogical References
5. **3Blue1Brown (Grant Sanderson).** *Essence of Linear Algebra.* 
   - Episode 1: "Vectors, what even are they?"
   - Episode 2: "Linear combinations, span, and basis vectors."
   - Geometric representation of linear systems as intersecting planes in $\mathbb{R}^3$.
6. **Boyd, Stephen, and Vandenberghe, Lieven.** *Introduction to Applied Linear Algebra: Vectors, Matrices, and Least Squares.* Cambridge University Press, 2018.
